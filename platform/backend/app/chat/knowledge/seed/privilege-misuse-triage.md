# Privilege misuse triage

## What it is
Privilege misuse is when an account uses access it legitimately has in a way that looks unusual or unsafe, for example an administrator reading data they have no reason to open. The access itself is allowed; the pattern is what stands out.

## Evidence to check first
Find who the account belongs to, what normal use looks like for them, and what was accessed and when. Check whether the activity matches a known task or change.

## What not to conclude
Unusual use of access is not proof of bad intent. Many cases are explained by a task nobody wrote down. Treat the first step as a question to the account owner, not an accusation.

## Containment options
Prefer the least disruptive step that fits, such as asking the owner to confirm. Restricting the account is a proposed response and follows the action policy and approval rules.

## After the alert
Record what the owner said and whether the activity was explained.
