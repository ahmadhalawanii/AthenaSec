# Benign alert triage

## What it is
A benign classification means the activity looks harmless after review of the evidence. It is a judgement, not a guarantee.

## Evidence to check first
Confirm the classification against the recorded events and the evidence listed on the alert. Check whether the detection model and the written analysis agree. If they disagree, say so and do not overwrite the stored classification.

## Closing a benign alert
Close it only when the evidence supports it, and note why. If the evidence is thin or missing, say the result is unconfirmed and leave it open for an analyst.

## What not to conclude
Do not treat a benign label as proof that nothing happened. Do not treat an empty alert list as proof the environment is safe.
